# Data, Confidentiality & Responsible Practice

## Permitted material
- Public information used within applicable licence and terms.
- Original analysis and frameworks.
- Clearly labelled synthetic datasets.
- Employer/client material only where explicit permission authorises public disclosure.

## Do not publish without explicit authorisation
- Confidential business information, client lists, contracts, pricing or internal strategy.
- Personal data or identifiable customer/employee records.
- Internal dashboards, screenshots, annotations or proprietary operational metrics.
- Credentials, access tokens, private keys or secrets.

## Synthetic data rules
1. Label simulated data prominently.
2. Document assumptions and generation logic.
3. Never imply simulated results represent a real company.
4. Use synthetic examples to demonstrate method, not to claim impact.

## Evidence and attribution
Cite external sources near supported claims. Record publication and access dates. Distinguish facts, calculations, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations. Respect copyright.

## AI use
AI may assist drafting or analysis, but the portfolio owner remains responsible for verification, accuracy, privacy and final judgement. Do not upload confidential information to AI services without authorisation and appropriate controls.

## Pre-publication checklist
- [ ] No secrets or personal data.
- [ ] No unauthorised employer/client information.
- [ ] Synthetic data is labelled.
- [ ] Claims are sourced or assumptions are explicit.
- [ ] Simulated outcomes are not presented as actual achievements.
- [ ] Third-party material is attributed and used appropriately.
