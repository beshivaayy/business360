# Business360 — Portfolio Charter

**Theme:** Business Growth, Operational Excellence & AI-driven Decision Making  
**Owner:** Ratnesh Patidar  
**Type:** Professional learning and demonstration portfolio

## Purpose
Demonstrate structured business thinking across management strategy, marketing and growth, operations and project management, and AI-enabled business strategy. This is a business portfolio, not a claim of software engineering expertise.

## Four pillars
1. **Management & Business Strategy:** business models, market assessment, competitor analysis, strategic options, business cases and risk.
2. **Marketing & Business Growth:** customer research, segmentation, positioning, go-to-market, funnel analysis, retention and account growth.
3. **Operations & Project Management:** process mapping, SOPs, RACI, delivery planning, capacity, SLAs, quality and KPIs.
4. **AI & Business Strategy:** opportunity discovery, readiness, prioritisation, workflow redesign, ROI, governance and adoption.

## Case-study standard
Each project should include an executive summary, context, problem statement, evidence register, analysis, options and trade-offs, recommendation, implementation plan, KPIs, risks, assumptions and limitations.

## Evidence integrity
Label facts, calculations, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations separately. Use public or clearly labelled synthetic data. Never publish confidential employer/client information or personal data without explicit authorisation. Do not present simulated outcomes as real achievements.

## Quality gate
Publish only when claims are supported or labelled, calculations are reproducible, recommendations are practical, risks are acknowledged, and the author can explain the work without overstating experience.

## Review cadence
Deliver one bounded, high-quality project at a time. Review the roadmap weekly.
