## What each case study contains

- Executive summary and decision/problem statement
- Scope, context, assumptions, and evidence register
- Analysis, options, and trade-offs
- Recommendation and implementation sequence
- KPIs, governance, risks, and limitations
- Clear distinction between proposed targets and measured outcomes

## How to read the work

These documents demonstrate a proposed management approach. They do not claim that the fictional organisation, baseline metrics, pilot, AI system, campaign, or business outcome exists. Where numbers appear, they are labelled illustrative. Replace assumptions with verified evidence before using a case study as a real-world result.
