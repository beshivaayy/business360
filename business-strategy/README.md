# Management & Business Strategy

**Core question:** Where should a business focus its resources, and why?

## Focus areas
- Business model and value-chain analysis
- Market sizing, attractiveness and competitor assessment
- Strategic diagnosis (SWOT/PESTLE as aids, not conclusions)
- Strategic options, prioritisation and scenario analysis
- Business cases, risk and capability requirements

## Portfolio outputs
Strategy memos, market assessments, options matrices, business model reviews and implementation priorities.

## First case-study candidate
A market-entry decision for a fictional B2B services company. Compare target segments using explicit selection criteria, public market evidence and clearly stated assumptions.
