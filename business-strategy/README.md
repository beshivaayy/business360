# Management & Business Strategy

**Core question:** Where should a business focus its resources, and why?

## Focus areas
- Business model and value-chain analysis
- Market sizing, attractiveness, and competitor assessment
- Strategic diagnosis (SWOT/PESTLE as aids, not conclusions)
- Strategic options, prioritisation, and scenario analysis
- Business cases, risk, and capability requirements
- Executive decision memos and stage-gate governance

## Featured case study
[STR-001 — Market-Entry Decision for a B2B Services Company](../case-studies/STR-001-market-entry-decision-for-b2b-services-company.md)

A synthetic decision framework comparing entry options, screening segments, assessing capability fit, and defining evidence gates before investment.

## Expected outputs
Strategy memos, market assessments, options matrices, business model reviews, scenario models, and implementation priorities.

## Quality standard
Distinguish market facts from estimates and hypotheses. State the geography, segment, time period, source, and methodology behind market claims. Test whether a recommendation changes when key assumptions or weights change.
