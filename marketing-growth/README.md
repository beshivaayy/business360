# Marketing & Business Growth

**Core question:** How can an organisation attract, convert, retain, and grow valuable customers?

## Focus areas
- Customer research, segmentation, and ideal customer profile
- Positioning and value proposition
- Go-to-market planning
- Customer journey and funnel analysis
- Campaign planning and measurement
- Client retention, expansion, and account growth
- Experiment design and learning agendas

## Featured case study
[MKT-001 — B2B Workflow Service Go-to-Market Plan](../case-studies/MKT-001-b2b-workflow-service-go-to-market-plan.md)

A synthetic commercial plan covering discovery, ICP hypotheses, positioning, offer design, channel tests, funnel qualification, and a 90-day execution roadmap.

## Expected outputs
GTM plans, segment prioritisation, journey maps, campaign briefs, funnel analyses, retention playbooks, and account-growth plans.

## Measurement standard
Define the denominator and time period for every rate. Distinguish leads, qualified opportunities, customers, active accounts, renewals, and expansion revenue. Do not claim campaign impact without a defensible baseline and attribution method.
