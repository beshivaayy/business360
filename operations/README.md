# Operations & Project Management

**Core question:** How can work be delivered reliably, efficiently, and transparently?

## Focus areas
- Current- and future-state process mapping
- Team coordination, SOPs, and role clarity (RACI)
- Project scope, milestones, dependencies, and risks
- Capacity and workload planning
- SLAs, quality assurance, and operational controls
- Root-cause analysis and continuous improvement
- KPI definitions, reporting, and review routines

## Featured case study
[OPS-001 — AI Data Annotation Delivery Workflow Improvement](../case-studies/OPS-001-ai-data-annotation-delivery-workflow-improvement.md)

A synthetic end-to-end operating-model proposal covering intake, allocation, annotation, QA, rework, and delivery. Includes a proposed SOP, RACI, KPI dictionary, pilot plan, and [blank KPI tracker](../templates/operations-kpi-tracker.csv).

## Expected outputs
Process maps, improvement proposals, SOPs, RACI matrices, project charters, delivery plans, KPI dictionaries, capacity models, and RAID logs.

## Measurement standard
For each KPI, document definition, formula, unit, source, owner, frequency, and limitations. Pair speed/productivity measures with quality and customer outcomes.
