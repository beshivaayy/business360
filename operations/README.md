# Operations & Project Management

**Core question:** How can work be delivered reliably, efficiently and transparently?

## Focus areas
- Current- and future-state process mapping
- SOPs and role clarity (RACI)
- Project scope, milestones, dependencies and risks
- Capacity and workload planning
- SLAs, quality assurance and operational controls
- Root-cause analysis and continuous improvement
- KPI definitions and review routines

## Portfolio outputs
Process maps, improvement proposals, SOPs, RACI matrices, project charters, delivery plans, KPI dictionaries and RAID logs.

## Measurement standard
For each KPI, document definition, formula, unit, source, owner, frequency and limitations. Pair speed/productivity measures with quality and customer outcomes.

## First case-study candidate
Improve a fictional AI data-annotation workflow from intake through assignment, annotation, QA, rework and delivery. Use synthetic volumes, not employer data.
