# Business360
### Business Growth · Operational Excellence · AI-driven Decision Making

A practical professional portfolio demonstrating structured business thinking across strategy, marketing and growth, operations and project management, and AI adoption.

> Portfolio exercises and simulated scenarios are labelled as such. They are not presented as real client engagements or employer results.

## Four pillars

| Pillar | Focus | Typical outputs |
|---|---|---|
| [Management & Business Strategy](business-strategy/README.md) | Strategic diagnosis and choices | Market assessment, business model analysis, options and business cases |
| [Marketing & Business Growth](marketing-growth/README.md) | Customer acquisition, retention and expansion | Segmentation, positioning, GTM plans, funnel analysis |
| [Operations & Project Management](operations/README.md) | Reliable execution and process improvement | SOPs, RACI, project plans, KPIs, quality and SLA frameworks |
| [AI & Business Strategy](ai-business-strategy/README.md) | Measurable, responsible AI adoption | Use-case prioritisation, readiness, ROI scenarios, risk controls |

## Case-study method

Every substantial project should explain: context, decision/problem, evidence, assumptions, analysis, options and trade-offs, recommendation, implementation, KPIs, risks and limitations. Start with the [case-study template](templates/case-study-template.md).

## Working principles

- Evidence before opinion; distinguish facts, calculations, assumptions and hypotheses.
- Practical recommendations with owners, sequencing and measurable outcomes.
- Public or synthetic data only unless explicit permission allows disclosure.
- No fabricated credentials, client work, revenue impact or operational savings.
- Responsible AI: privacy, security, human oversight, bias, reliability and total cost.
