# AI & Business Strategy

**Core question:** Where can AI create measurable business value, and what controls make adoption responsible?

## Focus areas
- AI opportunity discovery and use-case framing
- Readiness across process, data, people, governance and technology
- Prioritisation by value, feasibility, risk and time-to-value
- Build/buy/partner assessment
- Workflow redesign and human-in-the-loop controls
- Total cost of ownership and ROI scenarios
- Privacy, security, bias, reliability and vendor risk
- Change management and benefits realisation

## Portfolio outputs
AI opportunity assessments, prioritisation matrices, adoption roadmaps, business cases, risk registers and pilot scorecards.

## Responsible adoption
Start with a defined problem and baseline; do not automate an unknown process; retain accountable human review for consequential decisions; minimise and protect data; define monitoring, escalation and rollback; include integration, training, maintenance and oversight in costs.

## First case-study candidate
Assess AI-assisted quality review for a fictional annotation operation. Model time, review coverage, error escape rate, human oversight and total cost using synthetic data. Treat quality improvement as a hypothesis to test.
